Claude Stress

Presented by: David Hoese

Thursday, October 8 2026, 6:30 PM
Madison Public Library, 201 W Mifflin St, Room 301 [Map]
RSVP on Meetup

Software developers have found AI agents very useful. However, some developers have also found new levels of stress when working with these agents. In this MadPy discussion we'll be talking about how AI agentic coding has been affecting our mental state. Whether you're a multi-agent expert, an AI novice, or hoping to find time to learn this whole AI coding thing, join us and tell us how AI coding has changed your programming and how you feel while using it.

Note: This is a casual peer discussion among developers sharing experiences, not a substitute for professional mental health support.
