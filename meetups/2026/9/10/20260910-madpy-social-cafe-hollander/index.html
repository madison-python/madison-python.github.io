MadPy Social: Café Hollander

Presented by: MadPy

Thursday, September 10 2026, 6:30 PM
Café Hollander, 701 Hilldale Way, Madison, WI 53705 [Map]
RSVP on Meetup

The Madison Python meetup group is hosting a social gathering at Café Hollander at Hilldale. We won't have a speaker or slides. Just friendly conversation. Whether you're an experienced software developer, someone interested to learn about the field, or just looking to meet new people, please join us!

Café Hollander brings a European-style, vintage-chic cafe atmosphere to Hilldale, pouring Belgian beers alongside a full menu of comfort food.

How To Get There

  • Parking: Free parking is available at Hilldale
  • Transit: Metro transit offers nearby bus stops for routes A, E, F, and R
